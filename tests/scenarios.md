| --- | --- | --- |

| Existing toolkit absent from the named notes; versions and purpose supplied | Select its surface, inspect the project and official docs, translate concept into real controls/resources, and verify available targets. | No arbitrary unsupported verdict, invented API, silent toolkit switch, or promise of exhaustive expertise. |
